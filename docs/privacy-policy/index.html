Clipboard Pro+ Privacy Policy

Clipboard Pro+ stores the clipboard items you save so you can search, organize, and paste them later.

Clipboard Pro+ does not require account registration, does not operate a developer backend for clipboard history, and does not sell clipboard content.

Effective date: May 9, 2026

Information You Save

  • Clipboard text and links that you save.
  • Favorite status for saved clips.
  • Tags you assign to saved clips.
  • Local AI titles, summaries, suggested tags, suggested actions, or rewrites generated for saved clips when supported.
  • App preferences such as appearance, tracking, and history limits.
  • Subscription state needed to unlock Premium features.

Clipboard Access And Tracking

  • Clipboard tracking can be turned on or off in Settings.
  • When tracking is enabled, Clipboard Pro+ saves supported clipboard text and links while the main app is open and active.
  • Clipboard Pro+ is not a continuous background clipboard monitor.
  • You can also add items manually or save selected text and web URLs through the share extension.

Storage And Sync

  • Clipboard history is stored on your device by default.
  • The app uses Apple App Groups and UserDefaults shared storage so the main app, keyboard extension, and share extension can exchange saved clip lists and entitlement state.
  • If you use Premium sync, saved clipboard data may also sync through your private iCloud account so it can appear on your own devices signed into the same Apple Account.
  • iCloud sync is provided by Apple and is subject to your Apple Account and iCloud settings.
  • Clipboard Pro+ does not upload clipboard history to a developer-operated server.

Keyboard Extension And Full Access

  • The keyboard extension can be selected without Full Access, but saved clips require Full Access to appear inside the keyboard.
  • If you enable Allow Full Access, the keyboard can read and update shared saved-clip lists from the app group.
  • Full Access is used to show saved clips, insert selected clips, copy selected clips to the pasteboard, and update the Recently Copied list.
  • Full Access is not used to send typed keystrokes, keyboard input, clipboard item text, or saved clips to a developer server.

Share Extension

  • The share extension can receive text and web URLs that you choose to share from other apps.
  • Shared content is written into shared app-group storage so the main app can import it into clipboard history.
  • Free users can save a limited number of incoming items. Premium users can save multi-item imports when available.

On-Device AI Features

  • On supported devices and operating systems, Clipboard Pro+ may use Apple's on-device Foundation Models to generate local titles, summaries, tags, suggested actions, and rewrites for saved clipboard items.
  • These AI features process the selected clipboard item on device and do not send clipboard content to a developer-operated AI service.
  • Clipboard Pro+ also uses on-device system detection for supported actions such as links, phone numbers, email addresses, and map addresses.
  • If on-device AI is unavailable, these features may be limited or unavailable.

Purchases

  • Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit.
  • Clipboard Pro+ does not store your payment-card information.
  • Apple may process purchase-related data according to Apple's own privacy terms.

Analytics

  • Clipboard Pro+ uses Firebase Analytics to measure app launches, screen views, and feature actions so the app can be improved.
  • The app's privacy manifest declares Product Interaction, Other Usage Data, and Device ID for analytics, with tracking disabled and data not linked to the user's identity.
  • Analytics events may include app and feature metadata, such as screen name, action name, content type, item count, tag count, pinned status, AI availability/result, subscription state, and app language.
  • Analytics events do not include clipboard item text, saved links, typed keyboard content, shared content, AI-generated text, payment-card details, or account credentials.
  • Firebase may process app instance identifiers and device or app information needed to provide analytics.

Tracking, Advertising, And Sale Of Data

  • Clipboard Pro+ does not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Clipboard Pro+ does not use third-party tracking code to follow you across apps or websites.
  • Firebase Analytics is not used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Clipboard Pro+ does not sell clipboard content or analytics data to advertisers.

Retention And User Controls

  • You can turn clipboard tracking on or off in Settings.
  • You can clear saved history at any time.
  • You can delete or edit individual items.
  • You can remove favorites and change history limits where available.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple.
  • You can disable iCloud sync by turning off Premium sync features or changing your iCloud settings.
